# Pre-registration 5: prospective V. cholerae pooled-AF3 pilot

Written 2026-09-22, before any prediction was run. Jobs: `vibrio_pilot/jobs/` (85 pools, 3 AF Server
batches of <= 30 jobs; generated by `vibrio_pilot/design.py`, seed 20260922). Status: **designed, not
run.** It needs either the user's AF Server account (30 jobs/day, so 3 days) or Big Red 200.

## Design

- 24 competence/chitin-sensing proteins from *V. cholerae* N16961 (Dalia lab systems) plus 60
  random decoys (100-600 aa, excluding pilus/competence/chitin/secretion annotations). All
  3,486 pairs are covered at least once.
- Pools are capped at 4,000 aa: the largest size that fits Big Red 200's A100 40 GB, and equal
  to 5,000 aa on matched pairs (POSTHOC PH2).
- Default AF Server settings (templates up to 2021-09-30, 5 diffusion samples).

Each positive is classed *precedented* or *never-solved* by the same RCSB pipeline as H1 (pre-cutoff
co-complex of homologs), run before any prediction. The minor pilins (VC0857-VC0861) are reported
descriptively only, because their pairwise topology in *V. cholerae* is not established.

## Scoring (fixed)

S = mean over pools and the 5 samples of chain_pair_iptm, minus the Todor et al. size formula.
Null = the S distribution over all decoy-decoy pairs. Empirical p for a pair = share of null >= S.
**Hit** = p < 0.01. Bait rank = rank of the partner among the bait's 83 partners, averaged over
both directions.

## Predictions (from the audit)

1. **Precedented positives**: at least 4 of 6 are hits, and their median bait rank is <= 3.
2. **Never-solved positives** (expected: PilT-PilU, CBP-ChiS, DprA-ComM): descriptive only (n = 3).
   The audit predicts weaker recovery than for precedented pairs. These three were first proposed
   from AlphaFold models by the lab itself, so recovery here would be a best case for the class,
   not a general test.
3. **False-positive burden**: the share of system-decoy pairs that are hits. The audit's D1 suggests
   about 1 in 5 unprecedented hits is real, so a large system-decoy hit count would reproduce that
   burden.

The prediction outcome is reported as is. No rescoring, and no changes to the pool design after
seeing results.
