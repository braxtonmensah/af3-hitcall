Dear NIAID Preclinical Services team,

I am writing to ask whether an in vitro antimicrobial assessment against *Mycoplasma pneumoniae* falls
within the scope of the In Vitro Assessment of Antimicrobial Activity service, and what preliminary data
you would want before a request would be considered.

**The target.** *M. pneumoniae* RNase J (MPN280) forms a 2:2 heterotetramer with MPN621, a paralog that
has lost all four catalytic residues of the metallo-beta-lactamase nuclease active site. Both genes are
essential by transposon mutagenesis (Lluch-Senar et al. 2015, *Mol Syst Biol*), and MPN621 is essential
by colony isolation as well. The complex architecture is supported by AlphaFold3 (interface ipTM 0.84),
by in-cell crosslinks from two independent crosslinking chemistries (all six inter-protein crosslinks
satisfied in every model sampled, where the heterodimer leaves three at 51-63 A), and by published
size-exclusion proteomics placing both subunits in the same 298 kDa fraction, which matches the 2:2
prediction and excludes the 1:1 heterodimer.

**Why this interface.** MPN621 appears to be a structural subunit rather than a second nuclease, so the
protein-protein interface is the functional dependency. The nearest human relative is CPSF73, which
retains three of the four catalytic residues; the interface itself is only 24.6% identical to CPSF73,
so an interface-directed agent is the selective option and an active-site inhibitor is not.

**What I would be requesting.** MIC determination against *M. pneumoniae* for a small set of approved
compounds (on the order of 10 to 20), selected by structure-based virtual screening against that
interface. I would supply the compounds.

**My questions.**
1. Is *M. pneumoniae* within the scope of the current In Vitro Assessment of Antimicrobial Activity
   contract, or is the pathogen panel limited in a way that would exclude it?
2. What preliminary data would you consider adequate to support a request at this stage? I have
   structural and genetic target validation but no compound activity data yet, which I recognise is
   the gap.
3. Is a Nonclinical Evaluation Agreement required before a request can be submitted, and can it be
   executed with a small single-member LLC?
4. Roughly what compound quantity and purity would be needed?

I am an undergraduate researcher at Indiana University doing this work independently. All analysis is
pre-registered and reproducible, and I am glad to share the full record and the structural models.

Thank you for any guidance.

Braxton Mensah
bsmensah@iu.edu

## Notes before you send

- **Be upfront that you have no activity data.** Their published review criteria include "preliminary
  data adequate to support the request to advance the product". Asking what would be adequate is a
  better opening than pretending you already meet it.
- **Do not overstate.** No experiment has been performed. Every claim above is computational or from
  published data, and the email says so.
- **The IP question in item 3 is deliberate.** Their agreements are per organisation. Ask before signing
  anything as an IU affiliate.
