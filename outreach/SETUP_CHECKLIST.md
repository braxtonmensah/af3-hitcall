# Setup checklist, ordered by lead time (2026-09-25)

Everything here needs your identity, signature or money, so none of it is done. Ordered so the slow
items start first, because SAM.gov is what usually kills a first SBIR attempt.

## Start now (slow, free, blocking)

| # | Step | Where | Cost | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **SAM.gov entity registration** | sam.gov | free | **2-4 weeks, sometimes longer** | Needs the LLC's EIN first, so do steps 2-3 the same week. This is the usual reason a January deadline gets missed. |
| 2 | Name availability + **Articles of Organization** | inbiz.in.gov | **~$95-100** | same day online | Single-member LLC is fine. You are the sole member. |
| 3 | **EIN** | irs.gov (EIN Assistant) | free | instant | Do straight after step 2. Needs your SSN. |
| 4 | Business bank account | any bank | free-ish | 1 day | Bring Articles + EIN. Keep personal and company money separate from day one. |
| 5 | **SBC registration** (SBIR control ID) | sbir.gov | free | ~1 day | Required to submit. |
| 6 | **eRA Commons** account | era.nih.gov | free | 1-2 weeks | Register as the company; you are both PI and Signing Official. |
| 7 | Grants.gov registration | grants.gov | free | days | Needs SAM.gov active first. |

## Keep ownership clean while doing all of this

- No IU compute (Big Red 200, Quartz), no IU lab, no faculty-sponsored runs on this project.
- Move the repo off IU OneDrive to personal storage.
- Rented or personal GPU only for the Boltz-2 run.
- If you later want a faculty sponsor for the biology, expect IU to claim the IP (policy UA-24). That
  is a fine trade for a career, and a bad one for a company. Decide deliberately, not by accident.

## Ongoing

- Indiana biennial report, ~$32 every two years.
- An unfunded LLC with no activity still needs the report filed; set a reminder.
