# Funding ladder, written 2026-09-25

**Nothing here pays today.** No grant or fellowship reviews and disburses in a day. Anyone who says
otherwise is selling something. What follows is ranked by how fast real money can actually arrive,
crossed with how well Braxton's specific profile fits.

## The profile that matters

Undergraduate sophomore, no lab, no faculty sponsor, no funding, produced a pre-registered result that
survived five attacks plus a validated structural discovery, entirely on public data and a laptop. For
most funders that is a weakness. For a specific set of funders it is the exact thing they select for.
Earlier drafts in this folder aimed at YC and SBIR, which are both poor fits: YC wants a company and a
cofounder, SBIR wants an institution and six to nine months.

## Ranked

### 1. Z Fellows — the best immediate fit

`zfellows.com`. $10,000, rolling applications, open to students and people with jobs, explicitly built
so you do **not** have to quit school. That last point is why it outranks everything else here: it is
real money that costs him nothing structurally.

### 2. 1517 Fund, Medici grant

`1517fund.com/grants`. Minimum $1k grants to validate an idea, aimed at students and what they call
"SciFi science." Small, but fast and low friction, and 1517 explicitly backs students and deep-tech
scientists at the earliest stage. Worth doing in parallel with Z Fellows, not instead of it.

### 3. Emergent Ventures

`mercatus.tfaforms.net/5099527`. Rolling, $1k-50k, decisions typically in weeks. Draft already written
in `emergent_ventures_draft.md`. Highest ceiling of the three that do not require dropping out.

### 4. Thiel Fellowship — read this before applying

$250,000 over two years, no equity, rolling, open to anyone 22 or younger without a degree. On the
numbers it is by far the largest, and the profile fits.

**But the fellowship's premise is that you leave university.** That is not a detail to be negotiated
around, it is the entire point of the program. It directly contradicts two things already recorded as
his plans: the spring 2027 transfer, and the investment banking / private equity career goal, which is
a credential-gated path where leaving a target school is close to disqualifying.

So this is not a funding decision, it is a life decision wearing a funding decision's clothes. It should
not be applied to casually because the number is big. If the honest answer is that he wants IB/PE, then
Thiel is the wrong ladder and rows 1 to 3 are the right ones.

## What all of them gate on

Every route above improves sharply with two artifacts, and neither exists publicly yet:

1. **A public repository.** Currently private. The pre-registration discipline is the most credible
   thing about this work and it is invisible until someone can read `git log`.
2. **A posted preprint.** `PREPRINT_DRAFT.md` is now complete, all nine sections, no stubs. It needs a
   read-through in his own voice and a bioRxiv or arXiv posting.

Until those are public, every application says "trust me." After they are public, every application
says "check it yourself," and for a result whose whole selling point is verifiability, that difference
is most of the pitch.

## The shared pitch core

All four applications reuse the same three paragraphs, which is why the preprint matters more than any
individual form:

- Genome-scale AI screens propose more interactions than anyone can test, and users cannot tell which
  to trust.
- Measured it prospectively: confident predictions of never-solved complexes, frozen in 2021, were
  correct 81% (human, n=146) and 89% (yeast, n=84) against structures released 2022-2026, versus 2% for
  low-confidence. Survived homolog removal, a shuffled null, a cluster bootstrap, an independent metric,
  and a threshold grid. Pre-registered before the data were joined.
- Used it to find biology: an essential 2:2 RNase J heterotetramer in *M. pneumoniae* with a
  catalytically dead partner, supported by five independent published lines.

**Do not say "new protein fold."** RNase J is a known metallo-β-lactamase fold. The novelty is the
assembly architecture and the measured catalytic loss. The foldnovelty work already showed TM-score
novelty does not discriminate, so the inflated claim is one both false and self-refuted by his own data.
