# Pre-registration 27: are confident never-solved predictions functionally coupled? (CODEP)

Written 2026-09-25, **before any DepMap file was downloaded** and before any gene-effect profile was
joined to any predicted pair. The DepMap release is pinned to a fixed, citable version below so this
cannot drift.

## Why this test exists

Every validation in this repository so far is structure-adjacent, and each carries a structural
objection:

| Evidence | n | The objection |
|---|---|---|
| FUTURE (PDB entries released 2022-2026) | 146 | 126 of 146 are cryo-EM, and cryo-EM models are sometimes built starting from AlphaFold. The model may be agreeing with itself. The X-ray-only subset is 53% at n = 19 |
| XLVAL / XLDSS / XLHUMAN (crosslinks) | 15 human | Proximity is not interaction, and the human arm is tiny |
| TETRA / AFJ (RNase J) | 1 complex | One case |

**CRISPR co-dependency is immune to every one of those objections.** If two proteins act in the same
complex, knocking out either one tends to hurt the same cell lines, so their gene-effect profiles
correlate. That signal is measured by pooled CRISPR screens across ~1,100 cancer cell lines. It was
generated by cell biology, not by a structure predictor; no AlphaFold model can have contaminated it;
and it exists for thousands of pairs rather than dozens.

So this is the orthogonal, large-n, human, functional test the project does not yet have.

It also decides something commercial, which is stated here so the motive is on the record rather than
discovered later: **if confident never-solved pairs are functionally coupled, the 6,010-pair registry
stops being a list of unsolved structures and becomes a list of candidate protein-protein targets.**
If they are not, the registry is structurally interesting and functionally mute, and it should never
be pitched as a target list. Both outcomes are reported.

## Data, pinned before any download

- **DepMap 24Q4 Public**, `CRISPRGeneEffect.csv`, figshare DOI **10.25452/figshare.plus.27993248.v1**
  (published 2024-12-10). Chronos gene effect; rows are cell lines (ModelID), columns are
  `SYMBOL (ENTREZID)`. More negative means knockout hurts the line more. A newer quarterly release
  exists or will exist; this one is pinned because it is fixed and citable.
- **Burke et al. S1** as already used throughout this repo: `pDockQ`, `int3D_model_structure`,
  `Gen.id1` / `Gen.id2` (gene symbols), `CORUM_ID_selection`.
- Gene symbols are matched to DepMap column symbols exactly, after stripping the parenthesised Entrez
  id. Pairs where either symbol is absent from DepMap are dropped and counted. No alias resolution,
  because an alias table chosen after seeing results is a free parameter.

## Groups (fixed now)

All groups: human, two distinct UniProt accessions, both gene symbols present in DepMap, and the pair
is not a homodimer (`homodimers` flag false).

| Group | Definition |
|---|---|
| **T** (test) | `pDockQ > 0.23` and `int3D_model_structure == 0` — confident, never solved. The registry population |
| **L** (comparison) | `pDockQ < 0.10` and `int3D_model_structure == 0` — low confidence, never solved |
| **P** (positive control) | `pDockQ > 0.23` and `int3D_model_structure == 1` — confident and already solved, i.e. known complexes |
| **N** (null) | random gene pairs drawn from the same DepMap gene universe, 50,000 draws, seed 27, excluding any pair present in Burke S1 |

The middle band (0.10 to 0.23) is excluded, as in PREREG_HUMAN and PREREG_FUTURE.

## Statistic

For a pair, **r = Pearson correlation between the two genes' gene-effect profiles** across cell lines
with both values present. A pair needs at least **300 shared cell lines** or it is dropped and counted.

## Pre-specified filters, applied to every group identically

- **F1, variance floor.** Drop any gene whose gene-effect standard deviation across lines is below the
  25th percentile of all genes. A gene that barely moves has a correlation dominated by noise.
- **F2, paralog exclusion.** Drop pairs whose two proteins share **>= 30% sequence identity**
  (global pairwise identity on the UniProt sequences already stored in `burke/uniprot_seqs.json`).
  Paralogs co-vary through buffering rather than through complex membership, and they are also the
  pairs AlphaFold is most likely to model confidently for the wrong reason. Pairs where a sequence is
  missing are **kept**, and that count is reported.

## Tests

**C4 (instrument gate, read FIRST).** mean r in **P** minus mean r in **L**.
- The instrument is **sensitive** if this difference is positive with a 95% CI excluding 0.
- **If C4 fails, D1 is computed and reported but NOT interpreted**, exactly as PREREG_CLINVAR's failed
  positive control forced there. A test that cannot see co-dependency in known complexes cannot be
  used to argue about unknown ones.

**D1 (primary).** mean r in **T** minus mean r in **L**.
- Bootstrap over **proteins**, not pairs: resample the gene set with replacement and weight each pair
  by the product of its two genes' draw counts, 2,000 replicates, seed 27, 95% CI.
- **Supported** if the difference is positive and the CI excludes 0.
- **Refuted** if the CI excludes 0 in the negative direction.
- **Inconclusive** otherwise.

**C1 (essentiality-matched D1).** Co-dependency rises when both genes are essential, and confident
predictions may be enriched for essential, well-studied proteins. Stratify pairs into quintiles of
`min(mean gene effect of the two genes)`, recompute the T-minus-L difference within each stratum, and
report the sample-size-weighted pooled difference with its bootstrap CI. **D1 is only claimed if C1
holds in the same direction with a CI excluding 0.**

**C2 (CORUM sanity).** Report mean r for pairs flagged by `CORUM_ID_selection`, as a second reading of
the instrument. Descriptive; it gates nothing.

**D2 (the output, conditional on D1 and C1).** The pairs in **T** whose r exceeds the **95th
percentile of the null N**. For each: r, the two symbols, pDockQ, mean gene effect of each gene, and
whether either gene is pan-essential (mean gene effect < -0.5). This is the ranked list of pairs with
structural confidence, functional coupling, and no solved structure.

**Every pair in D2 is a hypothesis, not a target.** Co-dependency is correlational; two genes in the
same pathway correlate without touching.

## Refutation, stated before the result

If C4 passes and D1's CI includes 0 or is negative, then **confident AlphaFold predictions of
never-solved complexes are not functionally coupled by this measure**, and:

- the registry must not be described as a target list in any application, pitch or preprint;
- the corresponding sentence must be added to `RESULTS.md` and `STATE.md`;
- `MONEY.md` must drop any commercial line that depends on the registry being functionally meaningful.

That consequence is written down now so that a negative result costs something and cannot be quietly
reframed.

## Limitations, stated before the result

- **Co-dependency only sees complexes that affect fitness** in cancer cell lines. A real, correct,
  never-solved interaction with no growth phenotype is invisible here. Absence of co-dependency is
  therefore not evidence against an interaction, and D1 measures enrichment, not accuracy.
- DepMap lines are cancer cell lines. Tissue-restricted and post-mitotic biology is underrepresented.
- Gene effect is gene-level, so a pair whose interaction matters only for one isoform is blurred.
- Correlated profiles arise from pathway membership as well as physical contact. This test cannot
  separate those two, and D2 inherits that ambiguity.
- Symbol matching without alias resolution loses some pairs. That is a deliberate trade against a
  free parameter, and the loss is reported.
